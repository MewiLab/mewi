# Mewi — Design Philosophy

<br>

## Why

Many of us carry the quiet grief of good relationships that time pulled apart — not broken, just gone different ways. The friend who moved cities. The person you grew alongside, then gradually didn't. These weren't failures. They were real. And somehow that makes the missing harder.

Mewi is a place to sit with that feeling — and live it again.


## What
> Mewi is where you live that feeling again:
Each stray is like a different person you bond with — some take time, some come easy, each one different. <br>

Each has its own temperament. Some open up quickly. Some make you work for it. Some drift before you feel ready. 

They can bes influenced by your habits, shaped by your presence.

And then you get busy, stop showing up. Life pulls you both elsewhere. When you return, the closeness is already gone — no goodbye, just the quiet gap time leaves behind. You can try to rebuild with the same stray, or meet a new one and begin again.


## The Two Loops

<br>

```
[Just for now]
Short-term: >= 1 mins
Mid-term  : > 1 mins >= 10 mins
Long-term : above 10 mins
```

<br>

### 1. Short-term — the beginning of a bond, the thrill of being chosen

Each stray has its own personality. Some `play hard to get`. Some are `cautious`. Some are `bold`. You try to be chosen, to earn trust, to make a connection. `Just as you do with all kinds of people you meet in life`.

>*The stray does not comply. It considers. And when it chooses to acknowledge you, that moment feels earned — because it was.*

*The design elements: quick reflex according to personality, curiosity, not compliance. Unpredictable warmth, not programmed response.*

### 2. Long-term — the passing bond

Over time, a stray get `influenced by you`. It notices when you arrive. It remembers how you approach, what you offer, how patient you are. The bond deepens.

But the world keeps moving. If you are away, the stray interacts with other cats, other people, other things. Bonds drift the way living bonds do — not through betrayal, but through the passage of time and the pull of other lives.
Your importance fades naturally. The stray may not come to you the way it once did.

>*The departure was always coming. The bond was real anyway.*

## The Emotional Arc

```
Stranger → Interact → Start of Bonding → (Bonding) -> Drift → Departure → Remembrance
```

## What Mewi Is Not

- Not a pet simulator. The stray is never tamed, never owned.
- Not a sad game. The parting is gentle. The design holds loss without wallowing in it.
- Not a game about cats. It is a game about `the kinds of connection that time shapes and eventually separates.`
